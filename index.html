Methodology & caveats

This is a research-navigation tool, not an accusation index. It maps documented associations between named people/institutions and the major public collections of Epstein-related material, so you can jump to primary reporting and records. Node size reflects how prominently a name features in reporting — not any measure of culpability.

The five collections

Flight logs Passenger records from Epstein's aircraft entered as court evidence. Black book Epstein's contact directory (multiple versions leaked/auctioned). Court records Unsealed Giuffre v. Maxwell filings, depositions and exhibits (Jan 2024 and prior). 2025 emails The House Oversight estate-email releases (Nov 2025) and DOJ Transparency Act disclosures. Financial Bank suits/settlements, donations, trusts and Epstein's own entities.

What "documented" does and doesn't mean

A flight-log or black-book entry means only that a person traveled on the plane or that Epstein held their contact details. A name in an unsealed filing may be an allegation by an accuser, deposition testimony, a passing reference, or a person the witness said they never met. A name in the emails shows correspondence, not complicity. The July 2025 DOJ/FBI review concluded there is no incriminating master "client list." Settlements were made without admissions of liability.

Notable verification note

A widely-shared claim that Epstein spelled Jean-Luc Brunel's name phonetically as john look in an email could not be verified in reputable sources and is therefore not represented here as fact. (There is a separate, verified "phonetic nickname" email — the unrelated Bubba message between Epstein and his brother.) Some allegations in the record were later recanted by the accuser who made them. Deceased individuals could not respond to material unsealed after their deaths.

Sourcing

Entries are anchored to major outlets (CNN, CBS, NPR, PBS, TIME, BBC, Al Jazeera, Bloomberg, CNBC), court/DOJ/USVI records, and official releases. For a public-facing site, cite the underlying primary documents where possible. This dataset is a curated starting roster — not the full 3.5M-page corpus — and is meant to be expanded.
