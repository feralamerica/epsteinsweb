The Epstein Files, mapped. With receipts.

A plain-language, interactive map of the people, institutions, banks and documents that appear in the public Epstein record, with every entry linked to its primary source or established reporting.

Appearing here is not proof of wrongdoing. These files mix associates, business and social contacts, correspondents, staff and people named only in passing. This project exists to keep that distinction visible, and to make the underlying documents easy to check for yourself.

How to use this map

Reading the colors

Blue = person, orange = bank, purple = organization, green = university, yellow = trust/entity. Red overrides all of these and marks someone accused of involvement in the abuse (each profile notes denials). Amber squares are the five document collections; cyan diamonds are individual primary documents you can open.

Standards & methodology

This is a research-navigation tool, not an accusation index. It maps documented associations between named people/institutions and the major public collections of Epstein-related material, so you can jump to primary reporting and records. Node size reflects how prominently a name features in reporting, not any measure of culpability.

The five collections

Flight logs Passenger records from Epstein's aircraft entered as court evidence. Black book Epstein's contact directory (multiple versions leaked/auctioned). Court records Unsealed Maxwell civil case (2015) filings, depositions and exhibits (Jan 2024 and prior). Emails The House Oversight estate-email releases (Nov 2025) and DOJ Transparency Act disclosures. Financial Bank suits/settlements, donations, trusts and Epstein's own entities.

What "documented" does and doesn't mean

A flight-log or black-book entry means only that a person traveled on the plane or that Epstein held their contact details. A name in an unsealed filing may be an allegation by an accuser, deposition testimony, a passing reference, or a person the witness said they never met. A name in the emails shows correspondence, not complicity. The July 2025 DOJ/FBI review concluded there is no incriminating master "client list." Settlements were made without admissions of liability.

Notable verification note

A widely-shared claim that Epstein spelled Jean-Luc Brunel's name phonetically as john look in an email could not be verified in reputable sources and is therefore not represented here as fact. (There is a separate, verified "phonetic nickname" email, the unrelated Bubba message between Epstein and his brother.) Some allegations in the record were later recanted by the accuser who made them. Deceased individuals could not respond to material unsealed after their deaths.

Sourcing

Entries are anchored to major outlets (CNN, CBS, NPR, PBS, TIME, BBC, Al Jazeera, Bloomberg, CNBC), court/DOJ/USVI records, and official releases. For a public-facing site, cite the underlying primary documents where possible. This dataset is a curated starting roster, not the full 3.5M-page corpus, and is meant to be expanded.

How we verify

Aggregator sites (community wikis and trackers) are used only as leads, never as a citation. Every entry must be confirmed against a reputable independent source: a court or government record, an official release, or established investigative journalism. Where a claim rests on a single, uncorroborated source, it is held back rather than published.

What we exclude, and why

Victims, survivors and accusers are not listed on this map. They appear throughout the underlying files, but naming them here would serve no public-interest purpose and risks compounding harm. This is a deliberate editorial line, stated plainly so readers know what they are and are not seeing.

Allegations vs. facts, what the colors mean

Most people here are documented contacts, social, business, academic, financial or correspondence ties, and are shown in their category color. Red is reserved for the narrow set accused of involvement in the abuse or trafficking itself: named by an accuser as an alleged participant, criminally charged, or convicted. Even then, denials and outcomes are noted on the profile. Red means "accused," never "guilty."

Corrections & contact

This is a living document. If you can show an entry is wrong, mischaracterized, or missing context, or that someone has been cleared, it will be corrected or removed. Corrections are treated as a feature, not an embarrassment: accuracy is the entire point.
